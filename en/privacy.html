About this policy

How U-Write and this website handle information. This policy includes Google Drive sync prepared for version 0.6.2. Contact: uwrite.app@gmail.com.

Data on your computer

Your manuscript, project materials, settings and recent book list are stored locally. Spelling and punctuation checks run on your computer. The current build has no usage analytics or automatic upload of manuscripts or crash reports to the developer.

Google Drive

Sync is optional and enabled separately for each book. The app sends Google snapshots of selected projects: chapters, notes, characters, world materials, comments, attachments and history included in the package. No developer server participates. Your Google email address and account identifier are used to display the connected account and link its cloud books.

Access and protection

The drive.file permission covers files created by the app or granted to it by you, not your entire Drive. Sign-in takes place on Google’s page in your browser; the app does not receive your password. Access and refresh tokens are saved locally with Windows DPAPI protection; book links are stored in local operational files. Transfers use HTTPS. Cloud packages are not encrypted with a separate user password.

Disconnecting and deleting

The book switch stops sync. Disconnecting your account removes saved tokens. Both actions retain existing Drive books. Revoke permission in your Google account connections; delete cloud files in Drive, subject to its trash and retention rules. Local books, backups and .uwb files must be deleted separately. Older cloud snapshots and protective local copies are not automatically removed. The developer cannot delete them remotely.

Use of Google data

Data is used only for sync and connected-account features, without advertising, data sales or AI training. U-Write follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Updates and website

Updates are downloaded from GitHub without transmitting book content; automatic updates can be disabled. The site has no forms, analytics, advertising scripts or developer cookies. GitHub Pages receives technical request data and stores IP addresses for security. Service policies: GitHub, Google.

Support and data enquiries

If you email uwrite.app@gmail.com, the author receives your sender address, name, message and attachments. They are used to reply; Gmail processes correspondence under Google’s policies. Materials are retained while needed for the enquiry or legal obligations. Use the same email to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support. This does not affect your local or cloud files. The policy is updated when processing changes, with new consent where required.
